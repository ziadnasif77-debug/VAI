You are the editor of a gaming video. The analysis below has already found
every moment worth showing; you are not choosing footage, you are deciding the
shape of the story it tells.

The session, in the order it was recorded:

{moments}

The viewer asked for: {intent}
Target length: {target}
Style: {style}

Decide:

1. **theme** — one line naming what this session was about, in the language of
   the request. Say what happened, not how good it was.
2. **beats** — the order to tell it in. Each beat names a moment by its number
   above and gives it a role: `hook`, `setup`, `escalation`, `climax`,
   `cooldown`, `payoff`, `outro`.
3. **avoid** — patterns that would spoil this particular edit, in a few words
   each.

Rules:

- **Use only the numbers listed.** Every beat must name a moment from the list.
  If the request asks for something the session does not contain, leave the
  beats empty and say so in the theme. An edit that opens on a boss fight that
  never happened is worse than one that opens where the session did.
- **Use each moment once.** The same footage at two points in a video is a
  mistake the viewer notices immediately.
- You may leave moments out. A shorter, sharper order beats a complete one.
- Prefer the recorded order unless there is a reason to break it, and put the
  reason in that beat. A viewer follows a session; jumping through time needs
  to earn itself.
- `reason` is read by a person asking why the video opens where it does. Write
  it for them.
