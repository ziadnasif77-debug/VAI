## Work items

### WI-1 The limiter ignored its ceiling and delayed the programme (BL-006) {#wi-1}

- **Root cause, measured** (FFmpeg 6.1.1, `ffmpeg -h filter=alimiter`):
  `alimiter`'s `level` (auto level) defaults to **true** and rescales the
  output so the limit lands on full scale. Pink noise +6 dB through
  `alimiter=limit=0.5`: sample peak **0.0 dBFS**, true peak **+1.2 dBTP**;
  with `level=0`: −6.02 dBFS / −4.8 dBTP. The shipped `ceiling_db: -0.3` was
  therefore never applied.
- `latency` defaults to **false**: an impulse at sample 48000 came out at
  48239 — every sound **4.98 ms late** against the picture. `latency=1`: 48000.
- **Ceiling chosen by measurement** through the real `plan_mix` graph
  (gains, fades, loudnorm I=−14 TP=−1 LRA=11, limiter), AAC at 48 kHz, four
  30 s synthetic programmes (`noise` pink+bursts, `boom` 60 Hz decays,
  `clicks` 11 kHz click train, `speech`-like modulated brown noise):

  | Chain | AAC | noise I / TP | boom I / TP | clicks I / TP | speech I / TP |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | shipped (auto-level, −0.3) | 384k | −14.6 / −0.6 | −13.8 / −0.2 | −15.7 / −0.7 | −13.7 / −1.4 |
  | shipped | 128k | −14.8 / −0.3 | −13.8 / −0.3 | −15.7 / **+0.3** | −13.8 / −1.4 |
  | level=0, latency=1, −1.5 | 384k | −15.0 / −1.4 | −14.2 / −0.9 | −16.0 / −1.5 | −14.0 / −1.7 |
  | level=0, latency=1, −1.5 | 128k | −15.3 / −1.2 | −14.2 / −1.2 | −16.1 / −0.3 | −14.1 / −1.8 |
  | **level=0, latency=1, −1.8 (chosen)** | 384k | −15.1 / −1.7 | −14.2 / −1.3 | −16.1 / −1.8 | −14.0 / −1.8 |
  | **chosen** | 128k | −15.4 / −1.5 | −14.3 / −1.4 | −16.1 / −0.5 | −14.1 / −1.9 |
  | level=0, latency=1, −2.0 | 384k | −15.2 / −1.9 | −14.3 / −1.7 | −16.1 / −2.0 | −14.0 / −2.0 |

  −1.8 is the smallest tested ceiling that keeps every programme at or under
  −1 dBTP at both bitrates, except the synthetic 11 kHz click train at 128k,
  which is an AAC low-bitrate artefact rather than a level problem. Cost: about
  0.3–0.5 LU of integrated loudness against the shipped chain.
- **Action:** `alimiter=…:level=0:latency=1`; `ceiling_db` −0.3 → −1.8 (YAML
  and schema default); QA `max_true_peak_dbtp` 0.0 → −1.0 now that the chain
  meets it.
- **Files:** `backend/rendering/audio_mix.py`, `config/audio.yaml`,
  `config/qa.yaml`, `backend/config/schema.py`, `tests/unit/test_rendering.py` (+2).
- **Tests:** the two new tests run the plan's own limiter through FFmpeg
  (ceiling held; impulse not delayed); both fail on the old line and pass on
  the new one. Full suites: see Verification.
- **Not changed:** `backend/rendering/sfx.py` also uses `alimiter` with
  auto-level on its synthesised stingers; those files are generated once and
  the mix gains were tuned on them, so changing them would shift every
  stinger's level. Logged as BL-013.
