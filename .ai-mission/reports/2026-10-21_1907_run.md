### WI-2 ENG-001 — the shipped config fails off Windows <a id="wi-2"></a>
- **Problem:** `ApplicationConfig.media_source_roots` and `DailyConfig.output_directory/archive_directory` used `Path(...).is_absolute()`. The C-drive refusal also never fired on POSIX (`Path("C:/x").drive == ""`), and the archive field's error named `output_directory`.
- **Action:** `_is_absolute_anywhere()` (host rule OR drive-qualified Windows path; Windows behaviour unchanged, so a driveless `/x` is still refused there); drive read with `PureWindowsPath`; error names its own field.
- **Tests:** 3 new in `tests/unit/test_config.py`; all 3 fail on HEAD, pass after. `test_config.py` 34/34.

### WI-5 ENG-002 — the final limiter re-gains and delays the mix <a id="wi-5"></a>
- **Problem:** `audio_mix.py` ended the mix with `alimiter=limit=0.9661` (−0.3 dB). FFmpeg defaults: `level=1` multiplies output by `1/limit` (`af_alimiter.c:139`), and `latency=0` leaves the attack-time lookahead delay uncompensated (docs: "the delay it produces is the attack time you set").
- **Measurement (FFmpeg 6.1.1, synthetic pink-noise programme with bursts, 30 s, 48 kHz stereo):**

  | Chain after `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1:LRA=11` | I (LUFS) | Peak (dBFS) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | none | −18.0 | −1.0 |
  | `alimiter=limit=0.9661` (old) | −17.7 | −0.6 |
  | `…:level=0` | −18.0 | −0.9 |
  | `…:level=0:latency=1` (new) | −18.0 | −1.0 |

  Impulse at 1.000 s through the old limiter came out at 1.00498 s (5.0 ms late); with `latency=1` at 1.000 s.
- **Action:** `alimiter=limit=…:level=0:latency=1`.
- **Tests:** new `test_the_limiter_neither_re_gains_nor_delays_the_programme` (red on HEAD, green after); `test_rendering.py` 61/61.
- **Output check:** the slow e2e render (WI-8) produced h264 + aac, both streams start 0.000 s, both 36.000 s, −13.7 LUFS, peak −4.8 dBFS.
- **Commit:** `d807227`. **Status:** Verified and implemented (on synthetic and fake-model renders; not yet on a real recording).
